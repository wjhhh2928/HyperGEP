# Known limitations

Corrected-v1 is a candidate architecture with a new evaluation protocol. Historical PCC, transfer signs and importance rankings must be re-estimated before publication. Current training logs establish pipeline execution for the synthetic suite and a limited G2F excerpt. They do not establish performance on the paper cohorts.

CV fold variability measures split variability. A nonsignificant paired test cannot establish unbiased predictions or equivalence. Shared-parameter gradient conflict requires direct gradient diagnostics. Attention weights are predictive dependencies. SNP gradients are sensitivity measures conditional on the model and observed inputs.

DNNGP/DeepGS have locally runnable topology reconstructions. Original settings, portable historical LOEO results, full figures and formal asset staging remain incomplete. XGBoost, LightGBM and TabNet require optional dependencies. See the [release gate](../reports/RELEASE_BLOCKERS.md).
